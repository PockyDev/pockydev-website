# Privacy policy BrushPaw

Last updated: December 2026. The German version is legally binding.

## In short

BrushPaw works without internet, without an account, without ads and without analytics or crash reporting tools. The app stores your children's names, settings and brushing progress only on your device; I never get to see them.

## Controller

Tim Pockczerwinski, Heilsauring 73A, 23858 Reinfeld, Germany, e-mail: pocky_dev@web.de

## Who the app is for

BrushPaw is made for children aged 2 to 6. Parents set it up in the parent area, which is protected by a parent check. The app never asks children for any data.

## Data on your device

The app stores on your device:

- a child's name (optional), their sign (picture and colour), their brushing time and the allowed displays,
- the state of the daily tips,
- brushing progress: morning and evening per day for the 14-day view, the total and the earned costumes,
- app settings, the type of parent check and, if you set a PIN, the PIN only as a check value (salted hash), never in plain text.

The app does not transmit this data. When you delete a child, the app also deletes all of their entries. When you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android, everything is gone. The storage is technically necessary to run the app (section 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

## Backup and switching phones

BrushPaw is not backed up to the Google cloud. When you switch directly to a new phone (Android 12 or newer), Android can copy the app data to the new device by cable or direct connection.

## Permissions

The app only requests the “Vibration” permission (signal at the end of brushing).

## Feedback by e-mail

In the parent area you can send me feedback. The app opens your e-mail app with a prepared message. Only the subject, the app version and the Android version are pre-filled, never your children's names or progress. Nothing is sent until you tap “Send” in your e-mail app. I then receive your e-mail address and what you write, and use both only to reply to you. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (my interest in answering feedback). I delete the message once the matter is settled, after 12 months at the latest.

## Links to sources and the Play Store

The parent area has links, for example to the sources of the daily tips and to rating the app in the Play Store. They only open after the app has told you that you are leaving it. After that, the privacy rules of the opened site or of Google Play apply. Child mode has no links.

## Download via Google Play

When you download or update the app, Google Play processes data under its own rules. I have no influence on that. I only see crash and usage statistics from Google Play in summarised form, and only if you agreed to share diagnostic data in Android.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object (Art. 15–21 GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Independent Centre for Privacy Protection Schleswig-Holstein (ULD). Since I receive no data from the app, this practically only concerns e-mails to me.

## Changes

If the app changes, I will update this policy. The latest version applies.
